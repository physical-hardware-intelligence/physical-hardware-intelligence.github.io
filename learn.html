02Core papers · the policy family
PaperZhao · 2023

ACT / ALOHA↗

Action chunking: predict a burst of moves at once. The baseline every task gets first.

PaperChi · 2023

Diffusion Policy↗

Generate action trajectories by denoising. Handles the "many right ways" of a task.

PaperLee · 2024

VQ-BeT↗

Tokenize actions with a residual VQ-VAE. As expressive as diffusion, ~5× faster.

PaperShafiullah · 2022

Behavior Transformer (BeT)↗

Cluster behaviors into modes, let a transformer pick one. VQ-BeT's ancestor.

PaperFlorence · 2021

Implicit BC (IBC)↗

Score actions with an energy function instead of predicting them. The idea diffusion tamed.

PaperPari · 2021

VINN↗

No action network at all: good visual features plus a nearest-neighbor lookup over demos.

Paper2026 · we're testing it

Patch Policy↗

Keep dense vision-transformer patches instead of pooling them — beat a 7.6B model at 0.7% the size.

03Vision-language-action & world models
PaperGoogle · 2022

RT-1↗

The transformer that turned robot actions into tokens and trained on them at scale.

ProjectStanford · Berkeley

OpenVLA↗

An open 7B vision-language-action model. The reference point for "big VLA" baselines.

BlogPhysical Intelligence

π₀ — a generalist policy↗

A flow-matching VLA foundation model, explained in plain language. Read the blog, then the paper.

PaperHugging Face

SmolVLA↗

A 450M vision-language-action model that runs on consumer hardware. In our policy zoo.

PaperNVIDIA · 2025

GR00T N1↗

An open humanoid foundation model split into fast "acting" and slow "reasoning" systems.

CodeMeta

DINOv2↗

The frozen vision backbone Patch Policy — and half the field — builds on.
