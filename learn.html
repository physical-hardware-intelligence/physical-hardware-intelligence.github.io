SO-101Google DeepMind

MuJoCo Menagerie — trs_so_arm100↗

The official MJCF model of our arm. It ships two calibration variants — new-calib zeroes each joint mid-range, old-calib zeroes it fully extended. Pin one and write it down, or simulation and hardware will disagree about what an action means, silently.
