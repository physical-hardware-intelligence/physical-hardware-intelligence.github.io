Thirteen companies, one goal, no agreement on what to scale.

They are all building a robot foundation model. They disagree about what you have to scale to get one. Each bet buys something and costs something.

5 of the 13 sit on three axes at once, and that combination is the actual strategy. Physical Intelligence and Eka Robotics are the cleanest opposition on the board: same goal, and they disagree about where data comes from, what the model should predict, and whether generality costs you speed.

One absence is worth naming. Of these thirteen, only Toyota Research Institute publishes a statistically careful evaluation of its own model. The validation industry that does exist was built for autonomous vehicles, where somebody was legally obliged to prove a safety case, and it is only now moving into robotics.
