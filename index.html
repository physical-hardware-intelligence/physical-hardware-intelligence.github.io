      03THE ROBOT · SO-ARM101
    

    

      
Robot #00 — a 6-DOF arm you teach by hand.

      
Move the leader; the follower mirrors it. That's how we record the demos it learns from.

    


    

      

        
        
        
Tap a joint

      


      

        

          SELECT · JOINT
          
6 degrees of freedom

          
Six independent joints, each driven by a Feetech STS3215 servo. Tap any joint on the diagram to see what it does.

        

        

          
DOF6

          
ActuatorsFeetech STS3215

          
ControlLeader → Follower

          
VisionWrist + workspace cam

          
Software🤗 LeRobot

          
Cost≈ $370 assembled
